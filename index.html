Digital and IT: clear lanes

Digital owns

  • Customer-facing product strategy and roadmaps
  • UX standards, journey mapping, experience design
  • Digital revenue surfaces: registration, commerce, events
  • First-party customer data strategy
  • Innovation POVs and portfolio priorities

IT owns

  • Infrastructure and cloud operations
  • Network, security, compliance
  • Enterprise systems: ERP, HRIS, finance
  • Internal tooling and IT service delivery
  • Vendor and license management

Any tool that shapes a customer outcome, directly or through a rep, belongs on Digital's roadmap, with IT as the infrastructure partner.

How we work

How a request moves

SubmitDescribe the problem, who it affects, and the business impact. Please don't send requests to individual team members.
TriageThe product owner reviews new requests weekly and accepts, defers, or declines each one.
PlanAccepted work joins the product backlog. Bigger items get a one-page brief and a UX check first.
ShipBuilt in two-week sprints, shown at demo, released when it meets our definition of done.

Definition of done

  1. Meets acceptance criteria
  2. Passes QA and accessibility checks
  3. Analytics in place
  4. Release notes written
  5. Live for customers

The same standard applies to in-house, IT, and vendor work.
